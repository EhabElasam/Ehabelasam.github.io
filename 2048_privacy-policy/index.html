2048 Neon Privacy Policy

2048 Neon is designed as an offline game.

Data Collection

We do not collect, receive, sell, share, or use personal data from players.

Local Gameplay Data

The app stores only gameplay preferences locally on your device, including:

  • best score
  • current board state
  • sound setting
  • haptics setting
  • background and board theme settings

This data stays on your device. We do not have access to it, and it is not sent to our servers because 2048 Neon does not use our servers.

Third Parties

2048 Neon does not use accounts, analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, third-party tracking, or in-app purchases.

Platform providers, app stores, operating systems, device manufacturers, or services such as Google Play may process technical or diagnostic information under their own privacy policies. We do not control that processing and do not receive personal data from it through this app.

Future Changes

If 2048 Neon changes in a future version and begins using any service that collects data, this policy will be updated before release.
